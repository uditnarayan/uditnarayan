I'm currently a Member of Technical Staff at [DevRev](https://devrev.ai/), where I am part of the Infrastructure team. My work spans both foundational infrastructure and the development of shared platform capabilities that enable product teams to build, operate, and scale efficiently. While I continue to contribute to traditional infrastructure domains such as reliability, scalability, observability, and platform operations, my primary focus is on building ubiquitous platform abstractions that become foundational building blocks across the organization.

Previously, I served as Principal Software Engineer at [Atlassian](https://www.atlassian.com/) in the Forge Observability team within the Ecosystem Extensibility group. My work focused on designing and scaling observability systems for applications running on [Atlassian Forge](https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/forge/), the company's cloud app platform. I worked on challenges involving large-scale telemetry, monitoring, reliability, and platform visibility to help developers build and operate production-grade applications on Forge.

Before Atlassian, I was a Principal Software Engineer at [Bolt Financial, Inc.](https://www.bolt.com/), where I built highly scalable commerce and payments infrastructure. Prior to that, I was part of the Money & Payments team at [StockX](https://stockx.com/), working on critical financial systems that powered transactions at scale.

Earlier in my career, I served as Technical Lead at [HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/), where I helped build [Recruit](https://www.hackerearth.com/recruit/), the company's developer sourcing and technical assessment platform. I also spent several years at Infosys contributing to enterprise-scale knowledge management platforms including professional networking, enterprise search, knowledge sharing, and online learning systems.

My interests lie in distributed systems, platform engineering, observability, cloud infrastructure, developer productivity, and building reliable systems that operate at scale.
